Our Story

Stacey & Steve

What began as a home baking business has grown into two storefronts, but our commitment to quality remains the same. Using recipes passed down from Stacey's great-grandmother, we bake everything from scratch daily.

From our family to yours, every tart, bun, and cookie is made with the same love and care we put into our very first batch.
